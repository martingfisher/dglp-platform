Proposal for Doing Good Leeds Partnership

Spaces to hire

A new section of the partnership site where member organisations list rooms, halls and whole buildings for hire, and anyone planning a meeting, class or event in Leeds can find one. This page shows how it would look to a visitor.

  • Prepared by Results You Can Measure
  • Date 25 September 2026
  • Status Draft for discussion

  • One place to look

    Community centres, church halls and meeting rooms across the city, searchable by size, area, price and access needs.

  • Fits any set-up

    One room, a whole building, or twenty rooms across four sites. The same listing handles all three.

  • Built on what exists

    Members list spaces from the dashboard they already use, and the review team checks each one before it goes live.

How listings work

Three levels, so any venue fits

Every listing is built from the same three parts. A whole building is one more space at the venue, so an organisation can offer the lot, the rooms one by one, or both.

Enquire

  • Enquire stays in reach

    A bar at the foot of the screen keeps the price and the Enquire button in view while people scroll through the rooms.

  • Room cards stack

    Photo on top, then capacity, then price. The capacity grid keeps all four layouts on one line so rooms can be compared at a glance.

  • Held to the same standard

    Every button and link at least 44 pixels tall, text at WCAG 2.2 AA contrast or better, and no sideways scrolling. The same rules the member area and public pages are already checked against.

To decide

Six questions before we build

Each one changes the build. A recommendation sits beside each, so a quick yes is enough.

  • Who can list a space?

    Only verified member organisations, or any venue in Leeds that applies?

    Recommended

    Members only to start. They are already verified, their venue hangs off their directory page and it gives organisations another reason to join.

  • Enquiry or booking?

    Send an enquiry to the venue, or take bookings on the site with a live calendar?

    Recommended

    Enquiry first. Most venues already run their own diary, and a second calendar that drifts out of step causes double bookings. Bookings can follow once we know which venues want them.

  • Show prices?

    Should every space show a rate, or can venues keep prices private?

    Recommended

    Optional, with "Price on request" as the fallback. Listings with prices will rank higher in the results, which nudges venues to add them.

  • A member rate?

    Should venues be able to show a lower rate for partnership members?

    Recommended

    Yes, as an optional second price. It costs nothing to build and is a clear, visible benefit of membership.

  • A map?

    The map view needs a mapping service, which may carry a cost at higher traffic.

    Recommended

    Launch with the list and filters by ward, which cost nothing to run. Add the map in a second phase if people ask for it.

  • How much review?

    Should every change to a venue go back through the review team?

    Recommended

    Review every new venue and space. Let trusted organisations update prices, photos and availability without waiting, using the trust levels the platform already has.

Next step

Answer the six questions above, or talk them through with us. We will then write a scope with a fixed price and timeline for the first version: listing from the dashboard, review, the Find a space page, venue pages and enquiries.
