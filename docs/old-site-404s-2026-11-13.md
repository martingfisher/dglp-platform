# Old-site addresses that returned 404 (13 November 2026)

After go-live, Martin asked whether other old addresses were returning 404
besides the ones in the team's redirect sheet. This is what was checked, what
was redirected, and what is still a 404.

## How it was checked

The old site (now at `doinggood.wordifysites.com`) lists 5,445 addresses in
its sitemaps: 2,012 news and blog stories, 979 events, 1,794 training
courses, 149 pages, 23 volunteer stories and 488 archive addresses (tags,
authors, categories, locations). Every one of the 149 page addresses was
requested on the new domain. News stories were sampled only (22 tested: 11 answered 200, 6 returned 404, 5 were refused).
The test was stopped early because the host began refusing this machine's
requests again (403), so 16 of the 149 pages have no reliable result and
were treated as needing a redirect.

## Pages: 82 redirected, 10 left

57 of the 149 old page addresses already worked (same address on the new
site, or one of the sheet's redirects). 92 did not. A cheaper model (Claude
Haiku, run as a sub-agent) proposed a target for each from the list of the
new site's pages; Claude reviewed all 92, changed one (`/whats-happening/`
to `/news/`), and added 82 to the Cloudflare list `dgl_old_site_redirects`.
Each was checked at the edge with and without its trailing slash: 164 of 164
return a 301 to the address below.

"Confidence" is the model's own: high is the same subject, medium the
closest page, low a landing page because nothing closer exists.
